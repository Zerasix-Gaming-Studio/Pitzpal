# Pitzpal
Pitzpal is an open-source digital engine and interactive platform for South Indian and global Mancala traditions—including Pallanguzhi, Ali Guli Mane, Vamana Guntalu, and Kuzhipara—preserving their diverse regional rulesets, strategic depth, and cultural heritage through modern software and AI.

While Western Mancala variants like Kalah are widely digitized, traditional regional games—Pallanguzhi (Tamil Nadu), Ali Guli Mane (Karnataka), Vamana Guntalu (Andhra Pradesh & Telangana), Kuzhipara (Kerala), alongside cousins like Chongkak and Oware—feature intricate, distinct mechanics. From relay sowing and Pasu (capturing pits upon reaching four seeds) to multi-round progression and kattam (locked/rubbish pits), Pitzpal models these rulesets in a unified, extensible architecture.
